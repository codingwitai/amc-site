How it works

Everything happens over the internet. You never leave your chair, and we never touch your machine.

  1. You describe the problem

    Email us your basic specs and what's going wrong. We reply with an honest recommendation and a price — including "this isn't worth fixing", if that's the truth.

  2. You pay by card

    We send a secure payment link. The work is delivered over the internet and can't be returned, so payment comes first — then we schedule, usually within the hour.

  3. We scan and test

    Either we connect to your PC in a live remote session you watch, or you run our read-only scanner yourself and send us the log. It changes nothing on your machine unless you say so.

  4. You get the report and the plan

    Every subsystem scored 0–100 with the evidence behind each score, plus a numbered action plan where every step names the exact click path, what you should see, and how to undo it.

What we will not do — and why that's the point

  • We won't pretend to test your power supply. No software can honestly test a PSU. We report the rail voltages, the voltage sag under load, and the power-loss events in your system log — then tell you who can actually test it. Anyone who claims otherwise is selling you something.
  • We won't silently update your drivers. Automatic driver updates are the fastest way to break a working PC. We report what you have, what's current, and link you to the official vendor page.
  • We won't sell you parts. If you need hardware, we tell you exactly what to buy and you buy it from whoever is cheapest. We have no reason to recommend one over another.
  • We won't fix hardware over the internet. If the fault is a failed power supply, a swollen battery or a dead motherboard, we show you the evidence in the report and point you to a local shop with that report in hand. Our deliverable is the knowledge, not the screwdriver.

Questions

Do I have to bring my computer somewhere?

No. That's the whole idea. Arctic Morning Computing is a remote-only service — there is no shop, no counter and no drop-off. Everything is done over the internet, so you can be in Anchorage, anywhere else in Alaska, or anywhere in the world.

Is it safe to let you scan my machine?

The scanner is read-only. It changes nothing, installs nothing and sends nothing anywhere without your say-so. In a live session you watch everything on your own screen, and you can end the session at any moment.

Can you fix a slow computer without touching it?

Usually, yes. Most slowness is software — startup bloat, malware, a failing drive, an old driver, a power setting, a full disk, or thermal throttling from dust. All of that is diagnosable and most of it is fixable remotely. When the cause is hardware, we tell you plainly and point you to who can help.

What do I actually receive?

Three things: a scored report on every subsystem with the raw evidence behind each score, a numbered action plan where each step names the exact click path, what you should see and how to undo it, and a plain-English verdict on whether the machine is worth repairing or replacing.

What if my problem can't be fixed remotely?

Then you still get your money's worth: a written diagnosis with evidence, an exact list of what's wrong, and a clear recommendation on the repair or replacement. For hardware faults we point you to a local shop with the report in hand so nobody can tell you it's something it isn't.

How do I pay?

By card, through a secure Stripe checkout. You get an emailed receipt and invoice for every job. We don't take cash, and we don't take payment through Cash App, Venmo or Zelle — every job goes through an invoice so you have a proper record.

Where are you based?

Anchorage, Alaska. The business is 100% remote by design, so location doesn't limit who we can help — only the time zone does, and we work around that.

Not sure which service you need?

Send us your specs and a one-line description of the problem. We'll tell you exactly what we'd do and what it would cost — free.
